Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026  ·  BankMe by PAYPE Technologies Private Limited

This policy explains how refunds, failed transactions, and disputes are handled for wallet top-ups, transfers, and payouts made through the BankMe platform.

1. Wallet Top-Up (Add Money)

  • If a wallet top-up payment is deducted from your bank/card/UPI account but NOT credited to your BankMe wallet due to a technical failure, the amount will be automatically reversed to your original payment source within 5-7 business days, in accordance with your payment provider's (Razorpay/bank/UPI) reversal timelines
  • If the top-up is not reflected within this window, please contact admin@paype.co.in with your transaction reference ID for manual reconciliation
  • Successfully credited wallet top-ups are non-refundable to your bank account directly; funds must be used within the platform or withdrawn via the standard payout process

2. Failed Payouts

  • If a payout to your verified bank account fails due to a technical or banking error, the amount will be automatically credited back to your BankMe wallet
  • Payouts rejected due to name-mismatch (account holder name not matching your verified identity/business name) will not be processed; the amount will remain in your wallet
  • Payout processing times depend on the transfer mode selected (IMPS/NEFT/RTGS) and your bank's processing timelines

3. Failed / Duplicate Transactions

In the rare event of a duplicate transaction due to a technical error, please report it immediately to admin@paype.co.in with screenshots and transaction IDs. Verified duplicate charges will be reversed within 7-10 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Items

  • Convenience fees or transaction charges (where applicable) are non-refundable once a transaction is successfully completed
  • Wallet balance forfeited due to a confirmed Terms & Conditions violation (fraud, KYC misrepresentation) is non-refundable

5. Dispute Resolution Process

  • Raise a complaint via admin@paype.co.in with your registered mobile number, transaction ID, and a description of the issue
  • We will acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours
  • Most disputes are resolved within 7-15 business days, depending on the complexity and any third-party (bank/payment gateway) investigation required
