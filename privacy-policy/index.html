Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026  ·  BankMe by PAYPE Technologies Private Limited

This Privacy Policy explains how PAYPE Technologies Private Limited ("BankMe", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the BankMe mobile application and website (bankme.co.in). BankMe is a fintech platform offering digital wallet, KYC verification, and payment services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Identity & KYC Information

  • Full name, date of birth, gender (as per government records)
  • PAN (Permanent Account Number) and PAN card details
  • Aadhaar-linked demographic information (name, address, photo) — collected only with your explicit consent through UIDAI-authorized channels (DigiLocker / licensed eKYC partners). We do not store your full Aadhaar number; only a masked reference is retained.
  • Residential and business address

1.2 Financial Information

  • Bank account number, IFSC code, and account holder name (for payout verification)
  • Wallet balance and transaction history
  • Payment method details processed via our payment partners (we do not store full card numbers)

1.3 Business Information (for Retailer/Distributor/White Label accounts)

  • GST, MSME/Udyam, FSSAI registration numbers and verification status
  • Business/shop name, address, and registration certificates
  • Shop location (GPS coordinates) and shop photographs, captured at the time of registration for verification purposes

1.4 Device & Usage Information

  • Mobile number and email address
  • Device information (device model, OS version, unique device identifiers)
  • Profile photograph (captured via in-app camera for identity verification)
  • App usage logs, for security and fraud-prevention purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity and comply with RBI, UIDAI, and other regulatory KYC/AML requirements
  • To create and manage your BankMe wallet account
  • To process payments, top-ups, and payouts you initiate
  • To verify bank accounts before enabling payouts (including penny-drop verification)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, including comparing registered business location against device GPS location
  • To send OTPs and transactional notifications via SMS and email
  • To provide customer support

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with the following categories of trusted service providers, solely to deliver the services above:

  • Government eKYC gateways (DigiLocker/MeriPehchaan, UIDAI-authorized partners) — for Aadhaar and PAN verification
  • Business registry verification partners — for GST/MSME/FSSAI verification against government databases
  • Banking & payments partners — for bank account verification, UPI, and payment processing
  • SMS/Email gateway providers — for OTP and transactional communications
  • Cloud hosting providers — for secure data storage and application hosting

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access to sensitive KYC data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Aadhaar numbers are never stored in full — only masked references are retained, in compliance with UIDAI guidelines
  • We retain your data for as long as your account is active, and thereafter as required by applicable RBI record-retention regulations

5. Your Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the details below, subject to regulatory record-keeping requirements
  • You may withdraw consent for Aadhaar-based verification at any time; however, this may limit your ability to use certain services requiring KYC compliance
  • You may disable camera, location, or biometric permissions in your device settings, though this may limit app functionality

6. Children's Privacy

BankMe is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older, as required for financial KYC compliance. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the app or via email. Continued use of BankMe after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
